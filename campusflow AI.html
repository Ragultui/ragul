Why no live YOLO here? A hosted page can't download a detection model, so this build follows the deck's fallback plan: manual marking, or a sample scan that is labelled as sample data. The count-to-wait logic is the real formula: people ÷ service rate.

Assumptions & limits

  • Queue size is a rush-period signal, not a measurement of item sales.
  • Wait time assumes a steady service rate; real waits vary with image quality and queue boundaries.
  • No facial recognition. Images are not retained.
  • Demo data is illustrative. Staff stay in control of every decision.
